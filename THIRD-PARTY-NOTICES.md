# Third-party notices

`taurust` is a Rust port of an existing project, and its terminal experience is
derived from another one. Both are MIT licensed, and both notices are reproduced
below in full, as that licence requires.

## tau

`taurust` is a derivative work of **tau**, a Python coding-agent harness: its
architecture, its provider-neutral event contract, its message and tool
vocabulary, its retry and error policies, and its command surface are ported from
tau's `tau_ai`, `tau_agent` and `tau_coding` packages.

- Source: <https://github.com/huggingface/tau>

- Licence: MIT

## gentle-shell

The terminal visual language of `taurust` — the theme roles and palette, the
prompt chrome, the sidebar, the tool cards, the gauges and the todo panel — is
derived from **gentle-shell**.

- Source: <https://github.com/Gentleman-Programming/gentle-shell>
- Copyright (c) 2025 Mario Zechner
- Licence: MIT
